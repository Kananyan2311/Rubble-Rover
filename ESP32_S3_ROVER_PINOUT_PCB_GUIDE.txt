====================================================================================================
Target Architecture: Dual-MCU Central-Controller IoT System:
  1. ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1: Master Tactical Controller, WebApp Host, Sensor & Motion Hub
     - Controls 3x TB6612FNG Dual H-Bridge Drivers for 6 Independent High-Torque DC Motors:
       * Motor Driver #1: Front Pair (Front-Left & Front-Right)
       * Motor Driver #2: Middle Pair (Middle-Left & Middle-Right)
       * Motor Driver #3: Rear Pair (Rear-Left & Rear-Right)
     - Standby (STBY): Hardwired to 3.3V (Always Active, 0 GPIOs wasted).
     - Hosts Wi-Fi AP ("RubbleRover-Tactical-AP") & Embedded Tactical Web Server (192.168.4.1).
     - Acoustic Tracking: INMP441 24-bit I2S Digital MEMS Microphone (Real-Time Audio Streaming)
       * Pin-Minimization: L/R tied permanently to GND on PCB (0 GPIOs wasted; Left Channel).
       * Contiguous 3-pin physical bus on Header J3: SCK (GPIO 42), WS (GPIO 41), SD (GPIO 40).
     - Thermal Imaging: MLX90640 32x24 Far-Infrared Thermal Sensor (Live Heat Signature & Victim Alert)
       * Contiguous 2-pin I2C bus on Header J3: SDA (GPIO 2), SCL (GPIO 1) with 4.7k pull-ups.
     - Servos: 100% REMOVED from S3 (freed pins reallocated to sensors and clean layout).
     - Silicon Safety: Zero PSRAM conflicts, zero strapping traps, zero debugging interference.
  2. ESP32-CAM (AI-Thinker): High-Speed Optical Video Satellite
     - Connects as Wi-Fi station to ESP32-S3 AP (Fixed IP: 192.168.4.2).
     - Broadcasts fallback direct AP ("RubbleRover-CAM-AP", IP: 192.168.5.1).
     - Optical Video: OV2640 2MP Camera (MJPEG Live Stream on /stream at high FPS).
     - Tactical Searchlight: Onboard High-Power Flash LED (GPIO 4, toggled from WebApp/S3).
     - 100% relieved of external I2S and I2C sensors: zero pin conflicts, zero DMA contention!

3. INMP441 PIN-MINIMIZATION & I2S AUDIO INTERFACE (GPIO 42, 41, 40)
====================================================================================================

The INMP441 MEMS microphone provides 24-bit studio-grade digital audio.
To minimize pin count and eliminate wasted GPIOs:
1. The L/R (Channel Select) pin is hardwired directly to GND on the PCB:
   - When L/R = GND, the microphone transmits audio in the LEFT channel slot.
   - Saves 1 GPIO on the ESP32-S3!
2. Power is drawn directly from the 3.3V logic rail and common GND plane.
3. The remaining 3 signals are routed to three consecutive pins on Header J3:
   - J3-06 (GPIO 42) -> SCK (Bit Clock / BCLK)
   - J3-07 (GPIO 41) -> WS  (Word Select / LRCLK)
   - J3-08 (GPIO 40) -> SD  (Serial Data Out / DIN)

INMP441 HEADER PINOUT (Standard 6-pin 2.54mm header):
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Module Pin | Wire To                   | Description
-----------|---------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------
VDD        | 3.3V Logic Rail (J1-01)   | 3.3V Power Supply (Decoupled with 0.1uF capacitor)
GND        | System GND Plane (J3-01)  | Ground Return
L/R        | System GND Plane (J3-01)  | Hardwired to GND (0 GPIOs wasted; Left Channel)
SCK        | ESP32-S3 GPIO 42 (J3-06)  | I2S Serial Clock
WS         | ESP32-S3 GPIO 41 (J3-07)  | I2S Word Select
SD         | ESP32-S3 GPIO 40 (J3-08)  | I2S Serial Data Out

PCB ROUTING ADVANTAGE:
Because J3-06, J3-07, and J3-08 are contiguous, a 5-pin female connector (VDD, GND, SCK, WS, SD)
can be routed directly on the top layer with zero vias and zero signal layer crossings!

4. MLX90640 THERMAL SENSOR I2C INTERFACE (GPIO 2, 1)
====================================================================================================

The MLX90640 Far-Infrared sensor array contains 768 individual thermal pixels (32x24).
It operates over I2C at 400kHz (Fast Mode) or 100kHz (Standard Mode).

MLX90640 HEADER PINOUT (Standard 4-pin 2.54mm header):
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Module Pin | Wire To                   | Description
-----------|---------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------
VIN        | 3.3V Logic Rail (J1-01)   | 3.3V Clean Sensor Supply
GND        | System GND Plane (J3-01)  | Sensor Ground
SDA        | ESP32-S3 GPIO 2  (J3-05)  | I2C Serial Data (External 4.7k pull-up to 3.3V)
SCL        | ESP32-S3 GPIO 1  (J3-04)  | I2C Serial Clock (External 4.7k pull-up to 3.3V)

PCB ROUTING ADVANTAGE:
GPIO 1 (J3-04) and GPIO 2 (J3-05) are directly adjacent on Header J3, right above the INMP441 bus.
This keeps all sensor I/O together on the right header.

8. PCB LAYOUT & ROUTING DIRECTIVES (2-LAYER OPTIMIZATION)
====================================================================================================
1. Layer 1 (Top): Signal traces, point-to-point buses, and 3.3V logic rails.
2. Layer 2 (Bottom): Solid, unbroken Ground Pour (SYSTEM_GND).
3. Motor Trace Widths: VM, GND, AO1, AO2, BO1, BO2 traces must be at least 35-50 mil (or copper pour).
4. Audio & I2C Traces:
   - INMP441 SCK/WS/SD traces (GPIO 42, 41, 40) kept parallel with equal lengths (< 8 cm).
   - MLX90640 SDA/SCL traces (GPIO 2, 1) kept paired with 4.7k pull-up resistors close to the MCU.
5. Decoupling:
   - 0.1uF ceramic capacitors placed within 3mm of every VCC pin (TB6612, INMP441, MLX90640).
   - 100uF electrolytic capacitors placed right next to each TB6612 VM pin.
